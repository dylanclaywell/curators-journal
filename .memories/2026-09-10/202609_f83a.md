## Quest dataset generator: stages 1-3 built, cross-check earning its keep

`scripts/build-quests.ts` now fetches, parses, and cross-checks. Stage 4 (emit
`src/data/quests.json`) is the only one left.

### Stages 1-2 (committed: 20ba8ce, 8461e51)

- **Fetch:** inventory derived from `list=embeddedin` on the two infobox templates
  (196 quests + 20 miniquests), wikitext in 5 batched requests, cached to gitignored
  `.cache/wiki/` with revision ids. **Fails loud** — the deliberate opposite of
  `fetch-skill-icons.mjs`, since a missing quest silently drops out of the queue.
- **Parse:** brace-matching template extraction (not regex — requirement blocks nest
  several deep). 214 pages, 381 skill requirements, 276 prerequisite edges, 99 notes.
  Zero unresolved prerequisites.
- **TypeScript via `--experimental-strip-types`**, importing the app's real `SKILL_NAMES`
  and types so the generator can't drift from what the client consumes. Needed
  `allowImportingTsExtensions` plus `src/lib` in `tsconfig.node.json`'s include.

### Format variants found, beyond what the spike saw

The wiki writes an inline prerequisite **five** ways: "Completion of X", "Partial
completion of X", "Completed X" (past tense), "Started X", "Must have completed X".
Each was found by the cross-check flagging missing edges, not by reading pages.
Also handled: literal `requirements = None`, non-bulleted inline requirements,
`<!--editor notes-->`, `<ref>` footnotes, and `"is required for the following quests:"`
which is the **reverse** relation and would have inverted the graph.

### The bug worth remembering

While Guthix Sleeps says *"Ability to enter the Warriors' Guild (Attack + Strength >= 130;
OR Attack 99, OR Strength 99)"*. The parser took the first `{{SCP}}` on the line and
recorded **Attack 99 as a hard requirement** — telling a player they need 99 Attack when
they need a combined 130. Fix: **any line naming more than one skill is a disjunction, so
it becomes a visible note rather than a parsed requirement.** Ambiguity must degrade to
prose, never to false certainty. This is the same class of error as everything else this
project keeps guarding against.

### Validation

Graph is **acyclic**, deepest chain 8 (Song of the Elves) — so a dependency-ordered queue
is actually constructible. Cycles are a hard failure, not a warning.

### Cross-check results, categorized

Started at 72 disagreements, now 44, and categorizing mattered more than reducing:

- **20 ironman-only** — the module flags requirements that apply only to ironman accounts;
  pages keep those in a separate `ironman` param we don't parse. Counted separately so
  they don't bury real problems. **This is a genuine modelling gap** (see below).
- **8 structural** — the module splits Barbarian Training into sub-entries
  (Barbarian Fishing/Herblore/Smithing/...); the page aggregates them.
- **~6 derived** — the module lists Druidic Ritual explicitly wherever a Herblore level is
  required, since Herblore is impossible without it. Pages omit it as redundant. Harmless
  to omit: anyone with the Herblore level necessarily did the quest.
- **~7 genuine source disagreements** needing a human or in-game check: Watchtower Magic
  14 (page) vs 15 (module), Elemental Workshop I Mining 20 vs 30, The Knight's Sword
  Mining 10 vs 15, Tai Bwo Wannai Trio Fishing 5 vs 65 (the 65 is a `<ref>` footnote about
  catching karambwan yourself — our reading looks right), RFD/Freeing Evil Dave Cooking 25.

### Open decisions

1. **Ironman requirements are currently dropped entirely.** The app already supports
   ironman hiscores and account type is in settings, so for those players the dataset is
   incomplete. Needs a type change (`ironmanOnly` on `SkillRequirement`, or a separate
   list) plus parsing the page's `ironman` param — which is much proser than
   `requirements` and may only partly reduce.
2. Whether the ~7 real disagreements should block emitting or ship with a warning.
3. `requiredToStart` is unknown on 72 of 381 requirement lines; `boostable` on 12.
