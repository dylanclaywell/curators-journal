## Phase 3 spike: the quest dataset source is decided

Answered the roadmap's open question — which of three candidate wiki sources can back
`src/data/quests.json`. Measured all of them against the live wiki rather than guessing.

### Candidate 1 — Cargo tables: dead
`oldschool.runescape.wiki` has **neither Cargo nor SMW installed**. `action=cargoquery`
does not exist (`paraminfo` on `main` lists no cargo/ask/smw action). The roadmap listed
this first as the most attractive option; it simply isn't available.

### Candidate 3 — `Module:Questreq/data`: structured, but incomplete
A single hand-maintained 47KB flat Lua table backing `Template:Questreq`.

- 213 entries, 450 skill requirements, 317 prerequisite edges
- Parses cleanly with a brace/string tokenizer; **acyclic**, and all but 3 prereqs resolve
- **But it is 21 entries behind the canonical quest list**, and the gap is exactly the
  newest content: The Final Dawn, Sleeping Giants, Beneath Cursed Sands, Land of the
  Goblins, Architectural Alliance, plus a batch of Sailing-era quests. It also carries
  18 entries that aren't quests at all (8 achievement diaries, Tutorial Island,
  Barbarian Training sub-tasks).
- Data defects found: mixed tab/space indentation (so **indentation-based parsing is
  unsafe** — this cost one rewrite), one `'boosted'` typo for `'boostable'`, one
  `'Watchtower '` with a trailing space, 3 dangling prereq names.

Disqualifying on its own: using it silently omits ~10% of quests, biased toward the ones
a player is most likely to be looking at.

### Candidate 2 — per-quest page templates: chosen
The wiki's own quest lists are DPL-generated from per-page templates, so those templates
are the canonical source. `list=embeddedin` on `Template:Infobox Quest` (196 pages) and
`Template:Infobox Miniquest` (20 pages) yields the complete, self-maintaining inventory
and a **free quest/miniquest classification**.

Full wikitext for all 216 pages costs **5 batched API requests** (50 titles each).
Parsed with a brace-matching template extractor:

- `Infobox Quest` → name, number, members
- `Quest details` → difficulty, length, requirements
- `Quest rewards` → `qp`
- Coverage: 215/216 have difficulty and qp (only `Recipe for Disaster/Full guide`, a
  guide page, lacks both). 19 pages have no requirements block — these are the genuinely
  requirement-free early quests, matching the Lua module.

Requirements parse better than expected because they use templates, not prose:
`{{SCP|Skill|Level}}` + `{{Boostable|yes/no}}` + `{{Questreqstart|yes/no}}`. Prerequisite
quests are a nested bullet list under "Completion of the following quests:", where
**nesting is transitive expansion** — direct prerequisites are the shallowest tier, which
is a clean extraction rule. Yield: 396 skill reqs, 261 direct quest edges.

### Two findings that change the data model

1. **`{{Questreqstart|yes/no}}` means "required to start" vs "required to finish."** This
   is precisely the question the app exists to answer ("what can I actually start right
   now"), and the wiki already encodes it per-requirement. `SkillRequirement` needs a
   `requiredToStart` field. Annotated on 322/415 requirement lines (78%); `boostable` on
   384/415 (93%) — so both must have an explicit **unknown** third state, not a false default.
2. **~96 requirement lines are irreducibly free prose** ("The ability to defeat a level 83
   dragon", "Access to Mort'ton"). Not machine-checkable, so eligibility can never be
   fully computed from levels. They need to survive into the dataset as displayable
   `notes[]`, or the app will confidently say "startable" about a quest that isn't.

### Known messy tail, all small and bounded
- `Recipe for Disaster` accounts for ~52 of the unparsed lines: it lists subquests flatly
  without the standard header, and has subpages. Genuinely exceptional; handle as a special case.
- 4 one-off header phrasings ("Must have completed the following quests:") — a small regex widening.
- 3 miniquest pages yield a numeric `difficulty` (Alfred Grimhand's Barcrawl, Enter the Abyss, Family Pest).
- 2 prereqs need normalization (an underscore in an RFD subpage link; a Karamja diary reference).

### Impact
Phase 3 can proceed to `scripts/build-quests.ts` with a settled design: **page templates
as the spine, `Module:Questreq/data` as an independent cross-check** whose disagreements
get reported for hand-correction. That cross-check is worth keeping precisely because the
two sources are maintained by different people in different formats.

Spike scripts live in the session scratchpad (`probe2.mjs`, `fetch-pages.mjs`,
`parse-pages.mjs`); they are throwaway, and `scripts/build-quests.ts` is the real artifact.
